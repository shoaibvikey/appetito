OUR STORY

A good meal
is just the start.

Appetito Club brings Italian dining and a little outdoor adventure together in one easygoing place. It’s built for the long lunch, the family dinner, the post-ride pasta, and the “let’s stay a little longer” kind of day.

Gather around a table inside or out, share something delicious, then head for a lap on the pump track, a round of mini golf or a game of pickleball. However you spend the day, there’s always room for one more at Appetito Club.
